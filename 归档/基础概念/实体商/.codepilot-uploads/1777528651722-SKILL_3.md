# Earnings Call & Interview Analysis Skill V3.0

## Overview

Structured 8-dimension framework for extracting signals beyond literal content from management communications. Designed for buy-side due diligence on earnings calls, founder interviews, expert calls, and investor day transcripts.

Three methodological roots:
- **FBI/legal cross-examination**: Structural evasion, deflection patterns, topic truncation
- **Wall Street earnings analysis**: Guidance language shifts, precision changes, materiality filtering
- **VC due diligence**: Credibility tracking, narrative consistency, hidden intent

## Step 0: Input Classification (Do This First)

Before analyzing, classify the input material. This determines which sub-modules to activate.

| Input Type | Characteristics | Activated Modules |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Type A: Cleaned EC Transcript** | No filler words, polished grammar, often from IR services or third-party transcription | All 8 dimensions; **skip** emotion/tone sub-module in Dim 6 |
| **Type B: Raw Recording Transcript** | Contains 嗯/呃/啊, self-corrections, stutters, pauses marked | All 8 dimensions; **activate** emotion/tone sub-module in Dim 6 |
| **Type C: Meeting Notes / Summary** | Non-verbatim, condensed, may be from memory | All 8 dimensions but with **reduced weight** on Dim 3 (language signals unreliable in paraphrased text); focus on Dim 2 (evasion) and Dim 5 (numbers) |

If unsure, default to Type A.

---

## Framework A: Single-Period Analysis (8 Dimensions)

Apply sequentially. **Every claim must cite original text.** Each anomaly must pass a **Materiality Check**: Does this affect the core moat, primary profit driver, or near-term critical strategy? Only report Medium/High materiality items.

---

### 【1. 核心论点 · Core Narrative】

Identify the 3-5 messages management wants to convey. For each:

- **主动强调 vs 被追问才说**: Proactive = intent; Reactive = reluctant disclosure. The gap between these two categories IS the analysis.
- Rank by importance, cite original text.

---

### 【2. 结构性回避 · Structural Evasion】

Which questions were NOT directly answered?

| 分析师问题 | 管理层实际回答 | 回避方式 | Materiality |
|-----------|--------------|---------|-------------|
| (quote) | (quote) | (classify) | High/Med |

Evasion taxonomy:
- **转移** — Redirect to a safe topic
- **模糊** — Answer with vague ranges when specifics were asked
- **反问** — Deflect with counter-question
- **过度解释** — Filibuster: answer at excessive length on a tangential point to consume Q&A time. *Highly reliable even in cleaned transcripts — measurable by answer length vs question specificity.*
- **物理切断** — Moderator intervenes to move on, or management says "let's take the next question." *Highest signal — reveals maximum sensitivity.*
- **重新定义问题** — Reframe the premise before answering a different question
- **直接否认前提** — "We never said that" / "That's not how we think about it"

**Only report Medium/High materiality evasions.** A CEO dodging "what's your favorite product" is noise. A CFO dodging "what's the real same-store growth ex-delivery subsidies" is signal.

---

### 【3. 措辞异常 · Language Anomalies】

Identify with original text citations. Each must include a **"So What"** — why this specific shift matters to the investment thesis.

- **Precision shifts**: Specific numbers → vague ranges (downgrade signal) or vice versa. E.g., "grew 23%" last quarter → "grew double digits" this quarter.
- **Voice switches**: Active → passive when discussing negative outcomes. E.g., "We decided to slow expansion" vs "Expansion was slower than planned."
- **Self-correction mid-sentence**: Speaker starts optimistic, then walks it back. *The correction reveals the real position.* E.g., "We're definitely on track — well, the trend is generally positive."
- **New high-frequency words**: Words appearing 5+ times this call but absent from prior calls. Signals narrative shift. E.g., "健康" suddenly appearing 8 times when it was absent before.
- **[Type B only] Hedging word clusters**: Dense clusters of 基本上/差不多/应该/大概 around specific topics (not general speech patterns). Only flag if the density is **topic-specific** — some speakers naturally hedge everything.

**Do NOT flag**: General speech habits, cultural politeness markers, or filler words that are consistent across all topics.

---

### 【4. 叙事结构 · Narrative Architecture】

- **Who speaks first?** CEO opening = "story to tell." CFO opening = "nothing new, managing expectations." Both co-presenting = transition period or complex story.
- **Opening topic sequence**: The first 3 topics chosen reveal management's priority hierarchy. Compare to prior calls — did the order change?
- **Time allocation anomaly**: Disproportionate time on a minor topic often = deliberate filibuster to avoid harder questions later in Q&A.
- **CEO/CFO dynamic**: Does the CFO correct or qualify the CEO's statements in real-time? (Bullish signal for governance; reveals where CEO's optimism outpaces reality.)

---

### 【5. 数字一致性 · Number Consistency】

Cross-check all numbers against: (a) prior calls, (b) published financials, (c) internal logic within this call.

| 数字 | 状态 | 逻辑漏洞/备注 |
|------|------|--------------|
| (number) | ✅自洽 / ⚠️需验证 / ❌矛盾 | (explanation) |

Flag specifically:
- Numbers lacking stated methodology or source
- Numbers where precision suspiciously changes vs prior periods
- Numbers that "work" only under specific unstated assumptions
- **口径 inconsistencies** between public and private statements (if both available)

---

### 【6. 归因逻辑 + 情绪信号 · Attribution Logic & Tone】

**6a. 归因逻辑校验 (All input types)**

When management explains a miss, challenge, or underperformance:
- Is the cause attributed entirely to external/uncontrollable factors? (macro, weather, regulation, platform policy)
- **Peer check prompt**: "Did peers in the same market/period report the same external factor?" If yes → credible. If no → suspect.
- Is management taking ANY ownership of controllable failures? (Companies that never acknowledge mistakes are less trustworthy than those that do.)

**6b. 情绪/语气信号 (Type B raw recordings ONLY — skip for Type A/C)**

When working with raw recording transcripts that preserve filler words, pauses, and tone:
- Which topics produced the most animated/relaxed responses? (= genuine enthusiasm)
- Which topics produced defensive/terse responses? (= pressure points)
- Mismatches: Overly calm about bad news = pre-rehearsed. Overly excited about minor wins = compensating.
- Indicators: Filler word density spikes (呃/嗯/那个), sentence fragmentation, humor/metaphor usage, speaking pace changes.

**Important**: Do NOT infer emotion from cleaned transcripts. If the input is Type A, write "6b: Skipped (cleaned transcript)" and move on.

---

### 【7. 画外音 · Read Between the Lines】

Synthesize dimensions 1-6:
- What is the **real purpose** of this call? (Beyond reporting numbers — are they fundraising? Managing a downgrade? Pre-empting bad news? Building credibility for a future ask?)
- What does management **most want us to believe**?
- What do they **most want us NOT to dig into**?
- **One-sentence management state assessment**: Confident / Defensive / Anxious / Deliberately Optimistic / Transitioning Narrative

---

### 【8. 尽调推演 · Implication】

```json
{
  "assumptions_to_verify": [
    "Max 3 specific, testable hypotheses tied to Material evasions or consistency gaps"
  ],
  "followup_questions": [
    "Max 3 questions to ask in next meeting, ranked by information value"
  ],
  "risk_level": "green|yellow|red",
  "risk_reason": "One sentence focused on business impact, not text features."
}
```

- **Green**: Narrative consistent, commitments being met, no material evasions
- **Yellow**: Information gaps on material topics, or emerging pressure points, but no smoking gun
- **Red**: Material inconsistencies, credibility deterioration, or clear evasion of critical issues

## Framework B: Cross-Period Comparison

Run Single-Period (Framework A) on each call first, then apply this overlay.

### 【一致性检查 · Consistency Tracking】

| 主题 | 上期说法(引原文) | 本期说法(引原文) | 判断 |
|------|----------------|----------------|------|
| | | | 合理认知更新 / 刻意修正口径 / 记忆不一致 / 信息升级 / 选择性遗忘 |

### 【承诺兑现 · Promise Tracking】

| 承诺时间 | 承诺内容 | 兑现结果 | 偏差幅度 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| | | | +/-% or qualitative |

- Systematic under-promise → positive for credibility, but means published guidance needs a "discount factor" for modeling.

### 【新增风险词汇 · New Risk Language】

List expressions appearing in the latest call but **absent from ALL prior calls**. First-time defensive/cautionary language is high-signal.

### 【归因对标 · Peer Attribution Check】

When management blames external factors across periods, check:
- Did the same external factor appear in prior calls? (Recurring excuse = structural issue, not one-off.)
- Did peers cite the same factor in the same period? (If not → management-specific problem dressed as industry problem.)

### 【管理层可信度趋势 · Credibility Trend】

Score 1-5, with direction (变好/变差/稳定) and specific evidence from the consistency and promise tracking above.

### 【一句话结论】

One sentence on information transparency and management integrity trend.

## Framework C: Public vs Private Cross-Verification

When you have both public EC transcripts AND private meeting notes/interviews:

### 【口径差异 · Disclosure Gap Analysis】

| 主题 | 公开说法 | 私下说法 | 差异性质 | Materiality |
|------|--------|--------|---------|-------------|
| | | | 口径差异 / 信息分层 / 认知更新 / 矛盾 | H/M/L |

- **口径差异** (Technical): Same fact, different measurement methodology. Usually benign.
- **信息分层** (Deliberate): More specific/honest version given privately. Most common. **The GAP SIZE is the signal** — a 3% difference is normal; a directional difference (positive public, negative private) is a red flag.
- **认知更新** (Temporal): View changed between the two communication dates. Check if new information justifies the change.
- **矛盾** (Red flag): Directly contradictory statements on the same topic at similar time points.

### 【私下独有信息 · Private-Only Disclosures】

| 信息 | 内容 | 重要性 | 为什么只在私下说？ |
|------|------|--------|----------------|
| | | 🔴/🟡/🟢 | |

### 【公开独有信息 · Public-Only Rhetoric】

What was emphasized publicly but absent from private conversation? These are usually IR rhetoric — functional for market management, not information transfer. Their presence/absence helps calibrate what management considers "real" vs "performative."

### 【整体判断 · Overall Assessment】

- 管理层可信度: X/5
- 信息透明度: 公开 X/5, 私下 X/5
- 最大信息风险: One paragraph on the single most important thing you don't know but need to know.

## Framework D: Quick Analysis (5 Minutes)

For post-meeting rapid capture when there's no time for full analysis:

```
1. 最重要的3个信号（正面或负面）
2. 最需要追问的1个问题
3. 一句话总结：这次谈话之后，你对这家公司/这个人的判断变了吗？怎么变的？
```

No fluff. No repeating content. Only signals and implications.

## Quality Standards

| Dimension | Good Output | Bad Output |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| 回避识别 | Specific question + specific answer + specific evasion type + materiality | "管理层回避了某些问题" |
| 措辞信号 | Original text + "So What" tied to investment thesis | Checklist of hedge words without interpretation |
| 归因逻辑 | External blame identified + peer comparison result | "管理层归因于宏观" without checking if peers said the same |
| Implication | Specific, testable follow-up questions | "需要进一步了解" |
| 一致性 | Exact quotes from both periods + classified judgment | "整体较为一致" |
| 数字 | Cross-checked with specific prior references + 口径 noted | "数字基本合理" |

## Usage Notes

- **Language**: Match transcript language. 中文 transcript → 中文 analysis.
- **Bias check**: This framework is designed to find problems. Actively look for POSITIVE signals too — consistent delivery, increased transparency, genuine enthusiasm about the right things. A management team that acknowledges mistakes is MORE trustworthy, not less.
- **Context calibration**: A company's first-ever EC will naturally have more hedging than their 10th. A post-crisis call will be more defensive. Adjust sensitivity to context.
- **Multiple transcripts**: Cross-period comparison (Framework B) often yields more insight than any single-period analysis. When given 3+ transcripts, prioritize the comparison.
- **Private notes**: Often rough/abbreviated. Don't penalize for lack of polish — focus on substance gaps vs public statements.
- **Peer calibration**: When possible, compare management's claims against peers who reported in the same period. External attribution that is unique to one company (while peers thrived) is a stronger signal than industry-wide headwinds.
